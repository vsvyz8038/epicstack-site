Six ways we can help.

Each one is scoped in writing before work starts, so there are no surprises on cost or timing.

AI assistants and chatbots

Website assistants that answer customers from your own information, capture every lead, and hand over to a person when it matters. Set up, tested and maintained for you.

Good for: clinics, salons, institutes, agencies and any business that loses enquiries after hours.

Workflow automation

We connect the tools you already use so leads, emails, invoices and reports move on their own: a new enquiry comes in, a confirmation goes out, your team is told.

Good for: teams repeating the same copy-and-paste task every day.

Custom web apps and MVPs

Internal tools, dashboards and first versions of a product, built to a fixed scope so you can test an idea with real users before investing more.

Good for: founders and small teams with a clear problem and no engineering team.

Mobile apps

First versions of iOS and Android apps, scoped carefully so you can test the idea with real users before committing to a full build. We agree what goes in version one, and what waits, before any work starts.

Good for: founders who need an app and have no mobile developer.

Product consulting and MVP scoping

Ten years of product management applied to your idea: who it’s for, what to build first, what to leave out, and a written plan an engineer can build from.

Good for: founders unsure what to build, or holding a brief that’s still vague.

Website security health check

A plain-English review of your website’s basic security: headers, certificates, cookies and exposed files, with a prioritised fix list. It is a hygiene check, not a penetration test, and we only check sites you own.

Good for: small business and e-commerce sites.
